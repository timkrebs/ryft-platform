# Food data terms

Reviewed against provider documentation on 2026-11-24. Keep a provider, stable ID, release, source link and license with every imported record. Keep license notices and export tooling aligned with each release before distributing catalog data.

| Source | Terms and handling |
| --- | --- |
| USDA FoodData Central | USDA publishes FDC data under CC0 1.0 and requests attribution. Use “U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central” with the record link and release. [USDA reuse terms](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/), [USDA release downloads](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/) |
| Open Food Facts structured data | Database under ODbL 1.0 and individual contents under the Database Contents License. Attribute “Open Food Facts” with source and license links. Evaluate whether a public deployment creates a Derivative Database or Produced Work and how to offer the applicable derivative database or alterations under §4. Independent catalog entries remain traceable. Table separation alone does not decide whether public reuse triggers share-alike. [OFF reuse guidance](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/), [ODbL text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) |
| Open Food Facts product images | The provider describes a separate CC BY-SA license and warns of rights in depicted packaging. Verify the version and image-level rights, provide attribution and preserve applicable share-alike when reusing images. This importer does not ingest product images. [OFF image licensing](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/) |
| Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel | Max Rubner-Institut publishes BLS 4.0 under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. Keep the BLS code, release and link with the named attribution and mark modifications. The curated catalog may use matching BLS nutrient records separately from measured or estimated household portions. [BLS download and citation](https://blsdb.de/download) · [Version 4.0 DOI](https://doi.org/10.25826/Data20251217-134202-0) |

Before offering a combined catalog, document the public-use classification, attributions, export and licensing of the OFF-derived component, and verify that the export excludes RYFT private foods, submissions and health data. Reserve future translations and classifications for records with compatible provenance. For any external barcode fallback, use OFF's published product API limits and client-identification guidance. The current documentation gives 15 reads per minute per IP for product queries and 10 per minute per IP for search, with additional global protection. A fallback needs a configurable service-wide limit, circuit breaker, negative caching policy and disclosure of lookups. [OFF API usage and limits](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/)
