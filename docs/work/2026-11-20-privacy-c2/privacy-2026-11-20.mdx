## 2. What RYFT is

RYFT is an iPhone app. With your permission it reads data from Apple Health, sends it to the RYFT servers and computes recovery, strain, sleep and readiness from it. If you turn on saving workouts, it also writes your completed strength workouts to Apple Health (the type, start and end, the name RYFT and RYFT's identifier of the session; no heart rate and no energy). It is currently a free beta, distributed through TestFlight to invited testers only.

## 3. What we process

- **Your account:** an account identifier; your email address (with Sign in with Apple possibly a private relay address from Apple); your name, if you share it; how you sign in (Sign in with Apple, or email and password at Supabase); for Sign in with Apple a token, stored encrypted, which we use only to revoke the sign-in when you delete your account; the beta invite code you redeemed, if any; your time zone; when you last used the app.
- **Health data from Apple Health**, only the types you allow: heart rate, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), VO₂ max, active energy, steps, sleep (in bed, asleep, core, deep, REM, awake), workouts (type, start and end, average and maximum heart rate, energy, distance), the identifier Apple Health gives each record (so that no record is counted twice), and the names and bundle identifiers of the apps and devices that recorded them.
- **What RYFT computes from it:** daily scores (recovery, strain, readiness, sleep performance, need, debt, quality and consistency), step and energy totals, your personal baselines, insights, which of two recordings of the same workout counts, and which version of the scoring model computed them.
- **What you enter:** your birth year (to estimate your maximum heart rate) or a maximum heart rate you set, your sleep baseline, strength training (exercises, plans, sessions, sets, weights, body weight, personal records), your journal (for example alcohol, caffeine, nicotine, cannabis, supplements, feeling sick, injury, stress), and your notification settings. You can import training history and plans from another training app's export file: the file is read on your iPhone and is never uploaded; only the workouts and plans you confirm are sent, together with the custom exercises (their names) those workouts use, and each imported workout is stored with the name of the app the file came from. An import that has not been sent yet (paused, failed or waiting to be retried) is kept on your iPhone for up to 30 days and then deleted; signing out or deleting your account removes it at once.
- **Devices and notifications:** a random identifier per installation of the app, the device name, app and iOS version, when it last synced and a summary of that sync; the push token Apple assigns to the app, with its environment and language, and when a notification last reached it; which notifications were sent to you.
- **Device check (App Attest):** the app uses Apple's App Attest so that the RYFT servers accept only requests from the genuine RYFT app on a genuine Apple device. For this the app creates a key on your iPhone, and your iPhone has Apple attest it. RYFT stores, per account and installation of the app, the key's identifier, its public key, a counter, the environment, and when the key was attested and last used; the one-time challenges are stored only as a hash, valid for 5 minutes and deleted within the next 15. RYFT stores no receipt from Apple, no device model and no address. The app holds a token from the RYFT servers for up to one hour; the servers do not store it.
- **Your consent:** which version of this policy you consented to, when, with which app version, and when you withdrew it.
- **Product analytics, only if you agree:** if you turn on product analytics in the app, it sends a fixed list of events about how the app is used to PostHog (Section 5), under a pseudonymous identifier. ## 4. Why, and on which legal basis

- **Health data** (and the health-related parts of what you enter, such as the journal): to compute your scores and show them to you, on your **explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which the app asks for before the first upload. Without it RYFT accepts no uploads and computes nothing.
- **Account, settings, training, devices and notifications:** to provide the app you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Notifications only after you allow them in iOS.
- **Device check (App Attest):** to protect the service and your data against requests that do not come from the app, such as scripted access with a stolen sign-in (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). - **Product analytics:** to learn which parts of the app are used and where it fails, only on your **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Section 25(1) TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Logs, the firewall and rate limits:** to keep the service secure and running, to block attacks and abuse and to find server errors (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Feedback and emails to support:** to answer them and fix what you report (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
- **Invites and sign-in emails:** to give you access to the beta at your request and to let you sign in (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Consent records:** to be able to show that you consented (Art. 6(1)(c) with Art. 7(1) GDPR).
- **Website:** to deliver ryftlab.de (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 8. Security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). Each RYFT service runs under its own account, has its own database role and credentials and can reach only what it needs; keys and passwords are kept in Google's Secret Manager, in Belgium. The Sign in with Apple token is encrypted with a key that only the service that needs it can read. Data is stored encrypted at Supabase and Google. A firewall (Cloud Armor) and the device check above protect the servers. Only the operator has administrative access: to the admin tools (a sign-in with a second factor, or a key kept in Secret Manager), with every administrative request recorded, and to the database and the cloud projects. The admin tools show the operator account details (including email addresses, sign-in methods and the last sign-in, time zone and maximum heart rate), devices, data sources, consent and feedback, and summaries of how complete your data is (for example the number of days scored), but no scores or health values, so that the operator can help you and fix problems.

## 9. Age

You must be at least 16 years old to use RYFT.

## 10. No automated decisions

The scores are information for you. RYFT makes no decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects. RYFT is not a medical device.
