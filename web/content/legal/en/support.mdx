Write to [support@ryftlab.de](mailto:support@ryftlab.de), or report a problem from the app: Profile → About → Report a problem. You can also open it from the details of an error message, already filled in. Unless you turn off "Include diagnostics", the report carries the technical details we need to find the problem (the screen lists them before you send); it contains no health values. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says what is sent and how long it is kept.

## Signing in

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or with your Google account. RYFT has no password of its own, so there is no password to reset: if signing in fails, check your Apple or Google account first, then write to us.

## Strain, recovery or readiness stay empty

They need heart rate. An iPhone alone records steps, not heart rate: you need a watch or ring that writes heart rate to Apple Health. Check in the Health app under Browse → Heart → Heart Rate whether data arrives, and that RYFT may read it (Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → RYFT).

## The first days look odd

RYFT compares each night with your own recent nights, so recovery calibrates during the first 7 days. Set your birth year (or your measured maximum heart rate) in the app for a better strain.

## Data is missing

Open the app: it syncs on start, and in the background when iOS allows (Background App Refresh on). Data written to Apple Health later, for example by a watch that syncs late, arrives with the next sync.

## Export your data

In the app, open Profile and tap Export data, below the other settings. RYFT creates a JSON file with your account, your days, workouts and training sessions, and what you added by hand; save or share it. For anything else we store about you, such as your journal, write to us.

## Delete your account or withdraw consent

Delete your account under Profile → Account → Delete account. Deleting removes everything RYFT stores about you at once, ends your sign-in, revokes Sign in with Apple and, once connections exist, ends RYFT's access at Garmin or Suunto. Withdraw your consent under Profile → Privacy → Withdraw consent: uploads and scoring stop, and the data stays until you delete the account.

## Product analytics

RYFT sends no usage analytics unless you agree. Once the app offers it, you turn it on or off under Profile → Privacy → Share usage analytics; it is off until you turn it on.

## Garmin and Suunto

Today, data from a Garmin or Suunto watch reaches RYFT only through Apple Health: let the Garmin Connect or Suunto app write to Apple Health, and allow RYFT to read it.

Connecting your Garmin or Suunto account to RYFT directly is planned and not yet available. When it is, the app will show what RYFT reads before you connect, and you can disconnect at any time in the app or in your Garmin or Suunto account. Disconnecting deletes what RYFT received through the connection and ends RYFT's access at the provider; deleting your RYFT account does the same.

## Food diary

A food diary is being prepared and is not yet available to all testers. Its food catalog is compiled from U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central (Foundation Foods, SR Legacy and FNDDS), and from Open Food Facts (Open Database License). Once German staples are added, it also uses Max Rubner-Institut (2025), Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), Version 4.0, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0); RYFT selects values from it and converts them to its own units. Nutrient values are estimates; check the label where it matters to you.
