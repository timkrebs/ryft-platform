Write to [support@ryftapp.de](mailto:support@ryftapp.de), or send feedback from the app (Profile → About → Report a problem); feedback reaches us with the details we need to find the problem.

## Strain, recovery or readiness stay empty

They need heart rate. An iPhone alone records steps, not heart rate: you need a watch or ring that writes heart rate to Apple Health. Check in the Health app under Browse → Heart → Heart Rate whether data arrives, and that RYFT may read it (Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → RYFT).

## The first days look odd

RYFT compares each night with your own recent nights, so recovery calibrates during the first 7 days. Set your birth year (or your measured maximum heart rate) in the app for a better strain.

## Data is missing

Open the app: it syncs on start, and in the background when iOS allows (Background App Refresh on). Data written to Apple Health later, for example by a watch that syncs late, arrives with the next sync.

## Delete your account or withdraw consent

Both are in the app's settings. Deleting removes everything RYFT stores about you at once. Withdrawing your consent stops uploads and scoring; the data stays until you delete the account.

## Invite codes

The beta is invite-only. Codes come from the person who invited you.
