## 2. The service

RYFT reads data you allow from Apple Health and shows recovery, strain, sleep and readiness scores, training logs and a journal. Where it is available to you, it also keeps a food diary. It is a free beta distributed through TestFlight to invited testers: features change, and parts may not work as described. Connecting a Garmin or Suunto account directly is planned and not yet available.

## 3. Not medical advice

RYFT is a wellness app, not a medical device. Its scores, and anything it shows about food and nutrients, are estimates and no diagnosis. Do not use them to make medical decisions; ask a doctor about symptoms or before changing your training or your diet if you have health conditions.
